# No Black Box: Full Visibility & Control

**Unlok operates with complete transparency, showing you every file read and every proposed diff.**

Understand exactly what Unlok is doing and why—no obfuscation. Review all actions and approve changes before they're made. Unlok uses checkpoints, allowing you to easily revert if needed, maintaining full control over your codebase. Every turn shows which model handled it and what it cost, whether you're on auto-routing or your own keys.
